What this app does, and its limits

  • ✓Both voices travel over a real WebRTC call. Every spoken sentence is transcribed live and sent to AI, which reads the whole conversation like a quiet third person and scores it.
  • ✓People who genuinely know each other (family, friends) never trigger an alert, even when they talk about money or passwords. Only an unknown or suspicious caller who pushes for an OTP, PIN, app install or money does.
  • ✓An alert never ends your call. The warning plays (to you in your language, to the caller as a recorded voice), then the call carries on. Only the red End Call button ends it.
  • ~The synthetic-voice (deepfake) score stays low until you connect a voice-authenticity model. Speech-to-text can make mistakes, and AI can too: treat a warning as a prompt to be careful, not as proof.

Fusion formula

Every 0.25 s the signals (0–100) are combined into one fraud confidence:

F = 100 · σ( b + Σ wᵢ·scoreᵢ + w·stages − w·family )

Family warmth is discounted when the voice looks synthetic or when a hard ask is made, so a cloned voice cannot borrow trust.

When an alert fires

WATCH ≥ 30Quietly track; nothing is said. WARN ≥ 55Short whisper to the receiver only. INTERVENE ≥ 80Play the formal voice warning to each side. The call continues. Hard ruleAn unfamiliar caller asking for an OTP / PIN / CVV or a remote app, in a scam context, triggers at once. Hysteresis3 consecutive ticks to escalate, 6 to calm down: no flicker. Trusted contactSaved contacts need +10 more evidence. Known callersIf AI sees that you two genuinely know each other, no alert is raised.
